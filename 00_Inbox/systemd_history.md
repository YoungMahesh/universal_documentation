#### 2009 - Initial development

Lennart Poettering and Kay Sievers began working on the project, initally called Babykit.

#### April 2010 - Public announcement

Lennart Poettering published an article titled [Rethinking PID 1](https://0pointer.de/blog/projects/systemd.html), introducing systemd.

#### May 2011 - Fedora 15

Fedora became the first major distribution to adopt systemd by default

#### 2012 - Arch Linux

Arch Linux adopted systemd as its default init system

#### 2015 - Widespread adoption

Most major Linux distributions had moved towards systemd.
